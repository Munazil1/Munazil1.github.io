How I work

Start from the decision

Before building, I ask what the client needs to decide, then show a working version early and adjust to their feedback.

Code for rules, models for judgment

Anything a plain rule can decide never reaches the model. It's cheaper, faster, and easier to debug.

Plan for the model being wrong

Confidence scores, human review, and audit trails go into the first version, not a later one.

Deployment is part of the product

I've shipped into client infrastructure with restricted networks and replicated databases, and I treat that work as seriously as the model.
