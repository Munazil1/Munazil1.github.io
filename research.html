Research

Before industry work, I spent a research internship at DRDO building multimodal search, and published a paper on accessible email.

Maritime image search

Analysts needed to find surveillance images by describing them in plain language, but a standard CLIP model handled domain-specific maritime terms poorly.

I built a semantic image-caption search system on CLIP and PyTorch, with embedding pipelines, vector search, and re-ranking. Feature fusion and WordNet synonym expansion improved query-image matching by 15% over the CLIP baseline. The system runs fully offline, so it can be used on air-gapped machines.

Open-source version on GitHub
