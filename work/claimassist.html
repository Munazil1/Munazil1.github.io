ClaimAssist

Insurance claims intake and damage assessment

Takes in vehicle insurance claims from any channel, assesses damage with multiple AI models, and sends only uncertain or severe claims to a human reviewer.

4 channelsemail, WhatsApp, Telegram, live camera
2 modelsGPT for photos, Gemini for video
Every decisionwritten to a tamper-resistant audit log

The problem

Claims arrived through scattered channels and were assessed by hand. Results were inconsistent, and when a customer disputed an assessment there was no reliable record of how it was made.

How it works

  1. Take in the claim

    Claims arrive by email, WhatsApp, Telegram, or live mobile camera capture.

  2. Validate the vehicle

    OCR reads the number plate and VIN and checks them against policy records.

  3. Assess the damage

    GPT reviews photos and Gemini 2.5 Pro reviews 360° walkaround video for severity, repair cost, and fraud signals.

  4. Route by confidence

    Clear claims move ahead automatically. Uncertain or severe claims go to a reviewer.

  5. Record everything

    Each decision is written to an audit log, with a PDF summary and Slack or email alerts.

Amber steps are where a person makes the call.

What I built

The pipeline is orchestrated in n8n, with media stored in Azure Blob Storage and claim records in PostgreSQL. Each media type goes to the model best suited to it, and every assessment carries a confidence score that decides where the claim goes next.

Claim summaries are generated as PDFs automatically, and the team is alerted in real time when a claim needs attention.

Key decisions

  • The right model for each medium

    Photos and long walkaround videos have different needs, so each goes to the model that handles it best.

  • Validate before assessing

    Checking the plate and VIN against policy records first means damage is never assessed on the wrong vehicle.

  • People for the hard cases

    Confidence-based routing keeps reviewers focused on the claims that actually need their judgment.
